How the estimate works

A baby's first year splits into three very different kinds of spending: one-time gear (crib, stroller, car seat), monthly consumables (feeding + diapers × 12), and childcare — the single biggest line for most working families. We price each from 2026 US averages, then show where every dollar goes. Dive deeper in our budget breakdown guide and 2026 average cost guide.

Three levers that move the number most

  • Childcare type. Family care ($0) vs. a nanny (~$2,700/mo) is a ~$32,000 swing — bigger than every other choice combined.
  • Feeding. Breastmilk (~$30/mo in supplies) vs. premium formula (~$250/mo) differs by ~$2,600 over the year.
  • Gear tier. Secondhand basics (~$1,200) vs. premium brands (~$6,000). Babies outgrow gear fast — this is the easiest place to save.

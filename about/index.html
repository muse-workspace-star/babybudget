About BabyBudget

BabyBudget is a free set of baby budgeting tools: a baby cost calculator that estimates your first-year cost across gear, feeding, diapers and childcare, a budget breakdown guide showing where every dollar goes, and a 2026 average cost guide grounded in published surveys and retail data.

Why we built it

Most expecting parents start with one scary question — "how much is this going to cost?" — and get answers ranging from a forum guess to a retailer's sales pitch. We wanted a middle ground: transparent estimates built on published data, with the math shown (see our methodology).

What's free, what's not asked

  • Everything is free. No accounts, no sign-ups, no paywalled features.
  • No vendor kickbacks. We don't sell leads — the calculator recommends no vendors at all.
  • Estimates, not quotes. Every number on this site is a planning estimate. Real prices vary by city, childcare market, and brand — always collect real quotes before committing.

Who's behind it

BabyBudget is an independent project by a small team that builds free planning calculators. We don't publish personal contact details; the site speaks for itself — and every figure on it is traceable to a named source in the methodology page.

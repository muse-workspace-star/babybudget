Methodology & Sources

Exactly where our numbers come from, how the calculator does its math, and what the results do — and don't — mean.

Data sources

  • BabyCenter 2025 first-year cost research — average $20,384; ~$15,000 for budget-conscious families; $50,000+ with premium gear and full-time nanny care. Used as the national benchmark on our homepage and average cost guide.
  • Childcare prices — HHS/ACF Child Care Market Rate Survey 2025–2026 via 2026 analyses: center-based infant care $1,230/month, home-based $970/month; nanny ~$2,700/month. Care.com's 2026 Cost of Care Report corroborates ($332/week center infant; nanny starting rate ~$21.62/hr).
  • Formula prices — September 2026 US retail checks: Similac 360 Total Care $28.49 per 12.4-oz can, Enfamil NeuroPro $31.99, store-brand equivalents $18.99 (Target/Walmart). Parent-reported spending: ~$75/month store brand, $100–$150 name brand, $150–$250 premium/organic.
  • Diaper prices — $0.11–$0.45 per disposable diaper in 2026 retail; newborns use 8–12 per day, ~2,500–3,000 in the first year ($450–$1,200+); average ~$70–$80/month (National Diaper Bank Network). Cloth: $300–$800 upfront, saving $500+ over disposables.
  • Gear — The Bump estimates ~$2,000 for a nursery set (crib, changing table, rocker, dresser); car seats average ~$175; strollers range from a few hundred dollars to $1,000+.
  • Health insurance — KFF 2025 Employer Health Benefits Survey: average family premium $26,993, with workers contributing $6,850.

Calculator formulas

"Estimate first-year cost" mode. Three segments, summed:

  1. One-time gear: $1,200 budget / $2,800 mid-range / $6,000 premium (your pick).
  2. Monthly consumables × 12: feeding choice ($30–$250/month) + $25/month wipes & supplies + diaper choice ($45–$120/month).
  3. Childcare: care-type monthly rate × your months of paid care (0–12).

Each segment is rounded to the nearest $10; the displayed total is the sum of the rounded segments, so the breakdown always adds up. The per-month figure is the total ÷ 12.

"Allocate my budget" mode. Your entered total is split by the standard first-year shares — childcare 52%, gear & nursery 13%, feeding 12%, diapers & supplies 8%, health & insurance 6%, clothing 5%, contingency 4% — rounded to the nearest $10.

What "estimate" means here

  • Results are planning estimates built from national averages. They are not quotes, and no provider is bound by them.
  • Real prices vary enormously by childcare market (infant center care ranges $650–$2,400/month by state), brand choices, and family circumstances.
  • Daycare sticker tuition often excludes registration, supply fees, and holiday closures at full tuition; health-premium changes depend on your employer's plan.

Updates
